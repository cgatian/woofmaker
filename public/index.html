Make a “Buzz, your girlfriend… WOOF!” GIF

It's the best line in Home Alone: Kevin digs through Buzz's room, finds the framed photo of Buzz's girlfriend, and delivers the verdict. WoofMaker puts anyone in that picture frame: your friend, your sibling, your boss, your dog.

How it works

  1. Take a selfie or pick any photo from your phone.
  2. Drag and pinch to fit the face in the frame.
  3. Tap Make my WOOF!, then share the GIF to Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It's free, with no app and no sign-up.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The GIF is made right in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device.

How do I save it on iPhone?

Tap Share, then Save Image. On Android, tap Share or Save GIF.

What happened to the original woofmaker.com?

The 2013 original stopped making GIFs when its server went away. This fan-made version brings the holiday tradition back, rebuilt for phones.
